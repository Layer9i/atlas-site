What Atlas is

Your connection changes.Your presence doesn't.

ATLAS identity starts with the person, not the network, the device or the app. It gives you one identity that you hold, that follows you across Wi-Fi, mobile data and every device, and that sites can trust without anyone watching what you do.

The problem

The internet trusts your connection, not you.

Sites decide whether to trust you from where your connection comes from. Change networks, travel or try to stay private, and the services you use treat you like a stranger: extra checks, blocked logins, locked accounts.

And the tools meant to protect your privacy often make it worse. They send everyone out through the same shared addresses, so sites can't tell people apart, and the companies in the middle can see where you go.

What Atlas does

One person. One device. Multiple personas. Multiple geographic endpoints. One coordinated system.

Identity. Your personal internet identity is the foundation of ATLAS identity, and it is yours to manage independently of the internet you are using.

Personas. Create distinct personas for your internet activities, such as Business, Personal or Other. Each persona can have its own settings and geographic endpoints, so you manage different parts of your digital life independently.

Geographic endpoints. A persona can be mapped to multiple geographic endpoints. Change one persona's endpoints as needed, without changing the settings or endpoints of any other persona.

Identity Routing Fabric. ATLAS identity brings your personal internet identity, personas and internet pathways together, so you manage how your connections work from one account.

Our own network

An identity shouldn't have to borrow someone else's address.

Most privacy tools rent the same cloud addresses that sites already distrust. Layer9i is a registered network operator with its own network number and addresses, signed and mapped city by city, so the people behind them aren't lumped in with strangers.

Live today in Toronto, Montreal, New York, London and Amsterdam, with Vancouver, Chicago, Los Angeles and Frankfurt already reserved.

Day to day

Atlas Identity — investor overview

Person-controlled Internet presence. The mesh and exits make that identity demonstrable. They are not the product.

The ask

This raise stands up the first Atlas-origin IPv6 prefix: one colo, one transit session, RPKI.

Layer9i does not take customer payment until a third-party lookup shows that prefix. Sandbox commerce under Layer9i Inc is separate and charges no one.

  • Instrument: YC post-money SAFE via Stripe Atlas
  • Amount: $400k–$750k
  • Cap: $6M–$8M post-money
  • Use: one cage, cross-connect, and port, plus a v6 path and founder runway — not a seed-scale team
  • First payer: invited individuals on Presence. Independent relying parties come after the prefix is real.

Exact amount and cap are set in Stripe Atlas before any signed note.

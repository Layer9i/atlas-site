    Step by step

    What happens, step by step.

    Six things, in order, every time you use Atlas.

    1. 1

      You set up one account for all your devices.

      Your identity, your personas and your rules come with you to every device you own.

    2. 2

      You create your personas and set your rules.

      Business, Personal or Other, each with its own settings and geographic endpoints. You decide where your internet connections go, and the rules you set are the ones that apply.

    3. 3

      Each persona connects through the geographic endpoints you chose.

      On a network Layer9i owns and answers for, so sites see a network that stands behind the people on it.

    4. 4

      Every site gets its own separate ID.

      Each site knows you, but they can't compare notes about you.

    5. 5

      Atlas checks that sites really see the city you asked for.

      "Intended vs. Observed" is a real screen in the app: what you asked for, and what sites actually see.

    6. 6

      If Atlas can't keep its promise, it tells you.

      It steps aside so ordinary internet comes back, visibly. You are never left guessing.

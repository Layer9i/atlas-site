How it works

How Atlas works.

A VPN moves all of you to one place at a time. Everything you do leaves through addresses shared with strangers, and the company in the middle can see where you go. When you switch places, all of you switches.

Layer9i is moving beyond a traditional VPN into an Identity Routing Fabric. ATLAS identity is the engine behind it: one identity you hold, split into personas, each with its own city, all at the same time, on a network Layer9i owns.

    Step by step

    What happens, step by step.

    Six things, in order, every time you use Atlas.

    1. 1

      Your key is made on your own device.

      Layer9i keeps no copy. Your recovery phrase is the only way back in, and only you have it.

    2. 2

      You decide your personas and rules on the device.

      Which part of your life goes where is settled before a single thing leaves your phone or computer.

    3. 3

      Each persona leaves through the city you chose.

      On a network Layer9i owns and answers for, so sites see a network that stands behind the people on it.

    4. 4

      Every site gets its own separate ID.

      Your bank and your shop each know you, but they can't compare notes about you.

    5. 5

      Atlas checks that sites really see the city you asked for.

      "Intended vs. Observed" is a real screen in the app: what you asked for, and what sites actually see.

    6. 6

      If Atlas can't keep its promise, it tells you.

      It steps aside so ordinary internet comes back, visibly. You are never left guessing.

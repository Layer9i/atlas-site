The Identity Routing Fabric

October 22, 2026

Layer9i is moving beyond a traditional VPN into an Identity Routing Fabric. Atlas Identity is the engine behind it. Today the front page of layer9i.com says so, in plain words.

What a persona is

A persona is one part of your online life: your banking, your work, your shows, your shopping. Instead of broadcasting a single footprint, Atlas Identity lets you segment your digital presence into distinct, isolated personas, and give each one its own city.

All at the same time

A VPN moves all of you to one place, then all of you to the next. The Identity Routing Fabric is different. Your bank can appear from Toronto while your shows appear from London and your work from Montreal, all live together over one connection. One tap per persona, and the engine keeps every path running.

Personas never touch

Inside Atlas Identity your personas are completely separate. Your work, your money and your personal browsing never see each other, never share a connection, and never leak into one another. That is settled before a single thing leaves your device.

Pathways, across every device

Your pathways are yours, across one account for all your devices. Add your phone, your computer and the rest; remove one and nothing else changes. Your online footprint is dictated entirely by you, to the destinations you choose.

Also new on the site

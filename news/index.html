Atlas 101

How Atlas works, in four steps.

ATLAS identity gives you one identity that you hold. It follows you across Wi-Fi, mobile data and every device, and sites can trust it without anyone watching what you do.

1

Get the app

On your iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android phone or Windows computer. One plan covers every device.

2

Keep your key

You get a key when you sign up. It is the only way back into your account, and Layer9i never has a copy.

3

Choose your cities

Pick the city each part of your life appears from: banking from one, streaming from another.

4

Connect

Atlas shows Protected. Wherever you are, your bank, your apps and your accounts see the same you.

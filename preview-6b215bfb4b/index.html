What you can do with ATLAS identity.

Travel

Away from home, still you.

Choose the location you appear from, so the sites you use see you from your usual place. The home screen tells you when you are protected and where you appear.

Work and personal

Keep them apart.

A Business persona and a Personal persona, each with its own location. In Lens, send Personal through New York, Work through the Netherlands, and everything else through Toronto. Only the sites you list move; the rest stays on your normal connection.

One account

Your whole family.

Add a phone, a tablet or a laptop in a minute. Individual covers 2 devices. Family covers 5.

Clean DNS

Cleaner, and private by default.

We clean your DNS and keep no record of your lookups. Ad and tracker cleaning is in the app today.

A way out

Never stuck.

If Atlas cannot connect, it puts you back on the normal internet and tells you.

Our own network

The Layer9i commitment

We don't track you. We don't sell you.

Your internet presence belongs to you.

ATLAS identity is built to protect your connection and give you control over your online identity — not to turn your browsing activity into a product.

We don’t track your traffic.

Atlas keeps no record of the sites you visit or the apps you use.

We never sell your patterns.

Where you go online, when and how often is never sold, rented or shared. Not for advertising, not for anything.

Your data stays with you.

Your key and your rules are locked on your own phone and computer. Atlas can’t read them.

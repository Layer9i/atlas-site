ATLAS identity — the one-pager

Your presence. Your control.

In one line

One person. One device. Multiple personas. Multiple geographic endpoints. One coordinated system.

What Atlas does

Identity. Your personal internet identity is the foundation of ATLAS identity, and it is yours to manage independently of the internet you are using.

Personas. Create distinct personas for your internet activities, such as Business, Personal or Other, each with its own settings and geographic endpoints.

Geographic endpoints. A persona can be mapped to multiple geographic endpoints, changed without touching any other persona.

Identity Routing Fabric. The system that brings identity, personas and internet pathways together, managed from one account.

Built and running

  • Apps on four platforms: iPhone and iPad, Mac, Android and Windows, all on one identity.
  • Five cities live: Toronto, Montreal, New York, London and Amsterdam.
  • Layer9i's own internet network: a registered network number and address blocks, with signed routes, mapped city by city for location services.
  • An identity the person holds: created on their own phone from a recovery phrase only they have, approving each of their devices.
  • A separate ID for every site: a demonstration service already signs people in with Atlas.
  • Live subscriptions: sign-up, renewal and cancellation run end to end. Individual plan, up to 2 devices: $7.99 a month or $69.99 a year. Family plan, up to 5 devices: $11.99 a month or $129.99 a year. All prices are in US dollars. Launch offer, for a limited time: pay for your first month and get your second month free with any monthly plan.
  • Security checked in public: every server tested, account isolation proven on the live network, 7 of 7 issues fixed and published.

Business model

You

You at the centre, not the companies around you.

The network changes. You don't.

Home Wi-Fi, your phone's data, a hotel or a café: every network you join is different. With Atlas, your account, your choices and where sites see you connecting from stay the same on all of them.

Is Atlas for you?

Made for how you actually live online.

You're often away from home

Choose the city sites see you connecting from: Toronto, Montreal, New York, London or Amsterdam. Your bank can see you in the same place every time, whether you're at home, in a hotel or at the airport.

You use more than one device

Set things up once. Your phone, laptop and computer share the same account and the same choices. Change something on your phone and your laptop follows.

You're tired of being tracked

Atlas keeps no record of the sites you visit. Your choices are locked on your own devices before they reach us, so even we can't read them.

You want to own your account

Your account comes from a recovery phrase only you hold. Nobody at Atlas can reset their way in or take it over. The flip side: keep that phrase safe, because we can't get it back for you.

You want to know when something's wrong

Atlas checks that your connection is working the way you chose. If it can't confirm that, it's built to switch itself off, put your normal connection back and tell you.

You'd rather see the proof

We've published exactly what we could and couldn't hand over if a court ordered us to, including the one thing that is still a promise rather than impossible for us.

Already have a VPN?

A VPN hides your address. Atlas puts you in charge.

A VPN app sends your connection through its own servers, so sites see its address instead of yours. That's useful, but you're still trusting that company with your account and with whatever it decides to keep about you.

Atlas starts from a different place:

You hold the key, not us

Your account is yours from a phrase only you have. We keep no copy, so there's nothing for anyone to take from us.

We can't read your choices

Which sites you send where is locked on your devices. We store the locked copy and can't open it.

No record of where you go

We don't log the sites you visit, and the servers you connect through keep no record of which account used them.

Coming next: signing in to websites with Atlas, where each site gets a different ID for you so sites can't compare notes about you. It already works with our own test site. Real websites come next.

How it works

Three steps, then get on with your day.

  1. 1

    Install Atlas and write down your phrase

    Start on one device and write down the recovery phrase it shows you. Then add your other devices. Lose a laptop and your account isn't lost with it: your phrase brings it back.

  2. 2

    Make your choices

    Pick the city you usually want to appear from. Then add any exceptions, such as your bank appearing from home, or local sites using your own connection.

  3. 3

    Go about your life

    As you move between home Wi-Fi, your phone's data and hotel networks, Atlas keeps your choices in place and checks that they're working.

Privacy & data

Built so we're not the weak point.

We keep no record of the sites you visit, we don't connect your account to the internet addresses you use, and we can't read your choices. These are built into how Atlas works, not lines in a policy we could quietly change.
